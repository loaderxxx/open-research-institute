# Scope, search and editorial methods

[← Review contents](README.md)


**Review date: 18 November 2026.** This is an English-language, question-led rapid evidence review. It compares published human research relevant to a practicing gastroenterologist in Dubai. The team performed no new patient study, pooled effect estimate or individual-level reanalysis. Selection was purposive. Retrieval used web-indexed PubMed and PubMed Central records, journal sites, professional societies, official product information, and UAE sources. No assertion of complete database coverage follows from this search.

## Search and selection

The review combined older pivotal studies and guidelines with targeted 2025–2026 and September–November 2026 updates. Search families below capture the terms and filters used across the review; different search engines rank results differently. Online publication by the cutoff qualified even when the print issue was later.

| Domain | Representative search combinations |
|---|---|
| Clinical interventions | Recurrent CDI + microbiota + randomised trial + FDA label; IBD, Crohn’s, UC or IBS + FMT + placebo + remission; hepatic encephalopathy + recurrence + THEMATIC; liver transplantation + microbiome + outcomes |
| Prediction and testing | Clinical microbiome testing + international consensus; DTC + analytical performance + NIST; MASLD or NAFLD + advanced fibrosis + microbiome + FIB-4 + elastography |
| Endoscopy | FIT-positive + microbiome + prospective screening + advanced adenoma + 2026; Barrett’s + oesophageal microbiome + progression or surveillance |
| Nutrition and ageing | Postprandial targeting + Mediterranean diet + microbiome + randomised; obesity + weight regain; frailty, ageing or longevity + microbiome + placebo + clinical outcomes |
| Regional evidence | Emirati, UAE, Lebanese or GCC + microbiome + obesity; Saudi + treatment-naïve paediatric UC; Saudi aging microbiome; Abu Dhabi Department of Health + microbiome testing |
| Other public agenda topics | Resistant-organism carriage + sham + decolonisation; type 2 diabetes + metagenomes + metformin; pancreatic or breast cancer + microbiome + diagnostic accuracy; autism + microbiome + diet, validation or severity |

Recent direct comparisons, negative trials, external validations and Gulf cohorts received priority. Mechanistic speculation, product marketing and conference biographies do not establish efficacy. Conference abstracts and pilot studies are identified where they inform a live question. An association measured after diagnosis can reflect disease, treatment or diet; a change in microbial diversity was never treated as proof of a patient benefit.

## How to audit the claims

For efficacy, check who was randomised, who was analysed, what each group received, and the between-group clinical endpoint at the stated time. For prediction, look for the baseline comparator, independent validation, calibration and whether a result changes a decision. For a guideline, distinguish indication, jurisdiction and date. A trial conducted abroad and a UAE observational cohort play different roles in a UAE clinical decision.

| Example | Source material used in the cross-check | Why it mattered |
|---|---|---|
| Norwegian colorectal screening | [Full-text Table 2 and results](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13507204/) | Training-set and held-out results differ; overall neoplasia and invasive cancer yield different conclusions. |
| Italian screening feasibility | [Full-text methods and participant flow](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13413819/) | 315 screened, 236 eligible and 212 enrolled give different denominators. Feasibility is the endpoint. |
| November ulcerative colitis pilot | [Publisher abstract](https://doi.org/10.1093/ibd/izag236) | Changes within one arm and a between-arm symptom contrast are distinct estimates. |
| Abu Dhabi testing policy | [Official guideline, scope and effective date](https://www.doh.gov.ae/-/media/Feature/Resources/Guidelines/2025/Guidelines-on-Microbiome--2026.ashx) | Applies to the stated DoH remit. It cannot be extrapolated to authorisation in Dubai. |
| Nutrition | [Full-text 18-week trial and disclosures](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11271409/) | The programme uses several information sources; its trial cannot isolate the stool assay's added value. |

Reading depth varied between full-text sections, tables, regulatory documents, conference material and indexed abstracts. References in chapters lead to the inspected paper or its bibliographic record. Some paywalled articles could be assessed only through abstracts or accessible excerpts. Limitations include search ranking and language bias, the absence of exhaustive screening or independent blinded extraction, potential overlapping cohorts, and selective publication. No formal GRADE assessment or systematic retraction audit was conducted. The synthesis was AI-assisted and editorially checked; independent clinical peer review is pending.
