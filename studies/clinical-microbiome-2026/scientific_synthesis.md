# Clinical microbiome evidence and a review question for the UAE

[← Review contents](README.md)


Evidence checked on 18 November 2026. This is a targeted clinical briefing and a proposal for discussion. The search prioritised indexed human investigations, society guidance, official product indications and UAE primary sources. It is a narrative assessment. A registered protocol, duplicate screening and a formal risk-of-bias assessment would be needed for a systematic review. No access to clinics, datasets, patients or funding is assumed.

## What a test would have to change

A stool profile can identify differences between groups. To support an individual clinical decision, a test must first give reproducible results on the same material, then predict the specified outcome in the intended patients. Its added clinical value depends on whether it improves decisions or outcomes over existing care. The evidence required for an intervention is different again. A product that prevents recurrent infection after antibiotic treatment can succeed even if community profiling has no role in choosing which patients receive it. [International consensus on clinical testing](https://doi.org/10.1016/S2468-1253(24)00311-X) · [AGA guideline on fecal microbiota-based therapies](https://doi.org/10.1053/j.gastro.2024.01.008)

Three findings illustrate why these distinctions matter.

- **Measurement.** In a 2026 NIST-led study, seven direct-to-consumer services tested material from the same donor in triplicate. Between-provider variability was comparable in magnitude to the biological variation observed across donors. The study examined one standardised material and seven services. It establishes a reproducibility problem for those comparisons, without evaluating all clinical laboratories or the performance of a specific disease assay. [Servetas et al., 2026](https://doi.org/10.1038/s42003-025-09301-3)
- **Confounding.** Bowel habit and alcohol exposure can differ systematically between cases and controls. Matching on relevant host factors reduced apparent disease associations in a large human microbiome analysis. In a regional 2026 study of 43 Emirati and 30 Lebanese adults with BMI at least 35, estimates for nationality-associated genera weakened after adjustment for age. The groups also differed in adiposity and fibre intake. Geography, nationality and ancestry must not substitute for directly measured exposures. [Vujkovic-Cvijin et al., 2020](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-020-2881-9) · [Ali Ahmad et al., 2026](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41713465/)
- **Outcomes.** Microbial diversity, engraftment, a metabolite and a patient outcome answer different questions. A change in any of the first three needs evidence linking it to the stated clinical benefit. A 2026 multidisciplinary Delphi panel recommended specifying disease and safety outcomes in clinical microbiome trials, with harmonised clinical metadata. [Schierwagen et al., 2026](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41575-026-01229-1)

## Proposed first review

**In adults assessed for metabolic dysfunction-associated steatotic liver disease (MASLD), how accurately do prespecified stool microbiome signatures detect advanced fibrosis (F3–F4), what do they add to established noninvasive assessment, and which findings have independent validation in Gulf populations?**

The clinical decision is whether a patient needs further fibrosis assessment or hepatology referral. Existing pathways use a blood score such as FIB-4, followed by elastography when indicated. A marker would merit further study if it could detect important disease missed by the usual pathway, safely reduce low-yield referrals, or improve access at an acceptable cost. The published studies below did not demonstrate those outcomes. [EASL–EASD–EASO 2024 guideline](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jhep.2024.04.031) · [AGA 2026 clinical care pathway](https://gastro.org/clinical-guidance/clinical-care-pathway-for-the-risk-stratification-and-management-of-patients-with-masld/)

| Human evidence | Result | What the comparison establishes |
|---|---|---|
| Loomba et al., 2017. Single-centre prospective cohort of 86 adults with biopsy-proven NAFLD, 14 with F3–F4 and 72 with F0–F2. | A multifeature stool and clinical model had an area under the receiver operating characteristic curve (AUROC) of 0.936 in the development study. | Small number of advanced-fibrosis cases relative to features. The estimate supports testing for reproducibility in a separate, clinically representative cohort. [Primary study](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cmet.2017.04.001) |
| Lang et al., 2020. A German cohort included 83 biopsy-proven NAFLD cases plus 13 patients with cirrhosis diagnosed noninvasively. | AUROC 0.87 for their clinical-plus-microbiota model, 0.85 for clinical features alone, 0.85 for FIB-4 and 0.93 for transient elastography. An approximation to the previously published model yielded 0.71. | A modest numerical gain over the clinical model, with no proven change in referral outcomes. The attempted reproduction used a different analytical platform, and the reference standard was mixed. [Head-to-head study](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-020-66241-0) |
| Oh et al., 2020. Study cohort of 163 participants including non-NAFLD controls, people with NAFLD cirrhosis and first-degree relatives, with assessment in independent geographic cohorts. | Investigators reported an AUROC of 0.91 for cirrhosis detection and evaluated a signature combined with age and serum albumin in distinct cohorts. | Replication across sites strengthens the cirrhosis result. Distinguishing established cirrhosis from controls is a different clinical problem from identifying F3 among referrals. Evaluate these targets separately. [Primary study](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/32610095/) |

**Scope and extraction.** Search from database inception using both historical NAFLD/NASH and current MASLD/MASH terms, with microbiota, metagenomics, stool, fibrosis and validation synonyms. Include adult human studies that report a defined target stage and reference method. Record recruitment setting, spectrum of disease, number of events, clinical-only comparator, assay, locked or refitted model, reference standard, blinding and geographic independence. Separate discovery, internal validation and independent external validation. Treat biopsy, elastography and clinical definitions as distinct reference standards. Multicentre imaging and serum biomarker studies establish contemporary performance benchmarks. [LITMUS Imaging Study, 2026](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41591-026-04496-2)

**What would change a decision.** Prioritise sensitivity for advanced fibrosis, false referrals, negative predictive value at local prevalence, calibration and net benefit at a prespecified referral threshold. Capture costs, missing or indeterminate results, medication exposure, diabetes, age, body size, diet, stool consistency and recruitment site. Distinguish a patient-level independent test set from a split that allows repeated samples or household members to overlap. Summarise signed changes and uncertainty against the clinical comparator. Report the absence of eligible regional studies as a gap if a systematic search confirms it. Use PRISMA-DTA for reporting and QUADAS-3 for diagnostic-accuracy bias. Use PROBAST+AI to assess the risk of bias in multivariable models and TRIPOD+AI to assess reporting. [PRISMA-DTA](https://www.prisma-statement.org/dta) · [QUADAS-3, 2026](https://doi.org/10.7326/ANNALS-25-02104) · [PROBAST+AI](https://doi.org/10.1136/bmj-2024-082505) · [TRIPOD+AI](https://doi.org/10.1136/bmj-2023-078378) · [STORMS](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41591-021-01552-x)

## UAE interpretation and discussion

Abu Dhabi's *Guidelines on Microbiome Testing*, issued in December 2025 and effective March 2026, specify clinician involvement, informed consent about the limited evidence and accredited laboratory quality for providers in scope. The document identifies the entities it covers. Clinical activity in another emirate would require a separate check with the relevant authority. MBZUAI independently describes its Human Phenotype Project as a UAE longitudinal cohort incorporating microbiome and other data. Its 4,500-plus figure is a recruitment goal. No connection, endorsement or data access for this proposal has been established. [Abu Dhabi DoH guideline](https://www.doh.gov.ae/-/media/Feature/Resources/Guidelines/2025/Guidelines-on-Microbiome--2026.ashx) · [MBZUAI project description](https://mbzuai.ac.ae/research/project-hub/human-phenotype-project)

A first conversation can settle the target publication and clinical decision, the authors’ respective contributions, and whether the immediate deliverable is a literature review or a future clinical study. The literature review can proceed using public sources. Any discussion of UAE data or specific institutions should first identify the actual study sponsor, investigator, ethics jurisdiction and data custodian. The topic, collaborators and access remain proposals until confirmed.
