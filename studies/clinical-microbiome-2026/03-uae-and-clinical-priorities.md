# What Gulf cohorts can tell a Dubai clinic

[← Review contents](README.md)


Two kinds of evidence matter here. Gulf cohorts can reveal where an imported signature may fail. A clinical comparison can establish whether using a test helps UAE patients. The studies below contribute mostly to the first question.

## How the scientific priorities were selected

Dr. M. Azzam Kayasseh's Emirates Society of Clinical Microbiology presentation explicitly discusses microbiome-based diagnostics and treatments, IBS subtyping and an IBD risk-stratification module. The project slide names IBD, IBS, metabolic liver disease, diabetes, obesity, antimicrobial resistance, autism and colorectal, pancreatic and breast cancers. His IFCC WorldLab session addressed precision microbiome medicine. The RegenX programme lists longevity and describes his work in liver transplantation care. These documents establish public areas of interest. They do not establish the performance of a named score or imply involvement in this independent review. ## Local evidence, study by study

| Population and design | Finding | Clinical interpretation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| UAE, 2023. Stool profiles from 43 Emirati adults with obesity and 31 lean Emirati adults. | The obesity group had lower within-sample diversity. Associations with adiposity did not extend to measured fasting glucose or blood lipids. | A hypothesis about body composition. Individuals classified by an existing phenotype cannot establish prediction of incident metabolic disease. [Primary study](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37680528/) |
| UAE and Lebanon, 2026. Cross-sectional comparison of 43 Emirati and 30 Lebanese adults with BMI of at least 35 kg/m². | The cohorts differed in microbial composition, BMI and fibre intake. Adjustment for age weakened several associations attributed to nationality. | Applying one reference range across Arab populations would require validation. Both obesity publications include 43 Emirati participants with obesity. Their independence should be checked before pooling. [Primary study](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41713465/) |
| UAE Healthy Future Study, 2026. **Oral** profiles in 628 Emirati adults, including a multi-omics comparison of 97 participants with obesity and 95 matched controls. | Microbial and metabolite patterns co-varied with obesity and cardiometabolic markers. The underlying repository identifies baseline collection. | The mouth supplies a distinct signal. These results do not validate a stool score, demonstrate future event prediction or cover all Dubai residents. Individual data are controlled-access. [Primary study](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41576957/) · [Dataset description](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/projects/gap/cgi-bin/study.cgi?study_id=phs004453.v1.p1) |
| Saudi Arabia, 2024. A paediatric study compared 20 newly diagnosed, treatment-naïve patients with ulcerative colitis and 20 healthy controls. | The reported area under the receiver operating characteristic curve (AUROC) was 0.895 for all bacterial species and 0.976 using selected bacterial features. | The classifier separated the recruited groups. In a referral clinic it would have to distinguish UC from infections and other symptomatic conditions. Feature selection in a small dataset can inflate apparent performance. [Primary study](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38596448/) |

### How representative is the evidence?

A 2026 systematic review screened GCC microbiome literature through **31 January 2025** and included 110 studies across human, animal and environmental settings. Human studies comprised 49% of the total. Saudi Arabia contributed 44% of all studies, whereas Oman and Bahrain together contributed less than 7%. Most studies were observational. Those are publication shares across settings and cannot be used to estimate the proportion of Gulf patients represented in gut datasets. The 2026 human cohorts above fall outside the review's search period. [Regional systematic review](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42293552/)

## A testable local question

**Among consecutive symptomatic adult referrals to a Dubai gastroenterology service, does adding a prespecified stool signature to routine assessment improve selection for endoscopic evaluation of possible IBD?** This is a proposed review question. It is distinct from predicting future relapse in someone who already has IBD.

A first deliverable could use published aggregate data. Record the indication, reference diagnosis, symptom spectrum, comparator and decision threshold for each paper. Check whether a second site evaluated the original score unchanged. Compare the studies that used healthy controls with those enrolling diagnostically uncertain patients. The UAE consensus provides the local clinical pathway against which to assess any claimed addition. [UAE IBD consensus](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41024766/)

For an eventual local validation, define routine assessment with the clinical team and record drug exposure, diet, age, specimen type and recruiting site. Recruit across the population served by the clinic. Report calibration, false-negative referrals and extra endoscopies per 1,000 evaluated, with uncertainty. A model could discriminate cases well and still generate more unnecessary procedures than the current pathway at the chosen threshold. These are proposed design criteria, not findings from a patient dataset.
