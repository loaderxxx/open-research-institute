## Content and method

Pascucci combines social-ecological systems analysis with a meso-institutional perspective on translating general rules into practice and coordinating across levels. The article develops theory abductively through three partnerships—SAI, WCF and RSPO—selected from forty-two initially mapped cases. It therefore includes empirical cases, although it does not estimate partnership effects against a control group.

## ORI critical analysis

The connecting function deserves separate attention. Interpretation, verification and dispute resolution stand between a declaration and action. An organisation performing these tasks gains substantial influence even without formal sovereignty. ORI should identify to whom it is accountable and whether its rules can be challenged.

Selecting informative cases is appropriate for developing explanations, but cannot establish the prevalence of success. Longstanding international partnerships may differ from initiatives that dissolved or never emerged. Further comparison should seek cases of missing coordination to test which conditions are genuinely necessary.

Adopting a standard also differs from improving an environmental outcome. Agreed procedures may improve compatibility while retaining unequal representation of suppliers and local residents. Effectiveness and legitimacy require different criteria.

## Programme relevance

The article makes an intermediate level of voluntary governance available for investigation: who converts broad goals into verifiable responsibilities? ORI can apply this perspective to civic platforms and sectoral agreements while separately recording resources, authority and feedback. A useful test would trace a disputed episode from a general norm through decision and revision. This is an original research proposal, not a new evaluation procedure already established by the article. It would reveal where coordination depends on shared interpretation and where it depends on unequal bargaining power.

**Access and reading limits.** The abstract, theoretical framework and methodology including case selection were inspected. The article is open; the underlying case documents and findings were not independently checked.
