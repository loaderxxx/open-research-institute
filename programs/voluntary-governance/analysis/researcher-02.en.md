## Research direction

Arizona State University’s profile connects Eakin’s work with social-ecological resilience, adaptation, vulnerability and institutional conditions. A concrete programme-relevant example is her Cape Town water-provision study with Clifford Shearing, which uses interviews to examine city-business cooperation during crisis.

## ORI critical analysis

A strength of this direction is attention to how environmental events alter relationships between organisations and people. Drought does more than reduce available resources: it may change expectations, duties and opportunities to refuse. Voluntariness should therefore be investigated as a property of specific interactions rather than a permanent organisational characteristic.

A qualitative case helps reconstruct decision sequences and participants’ different interpretations. Assessing consequences requires combining it with independent evidence on access, service quality and cost distribution. Successful coordination between influential organisations does not establish that vulnerable users were protected.

Further reading should distinguish system resilience from preserving an existing order. Restoring services may reproduce earlier inequalities. Improvement sometimes requires changing rules rather than returning to an initial condition. This normative choice needs to be explicit so resilience does not conceal competing objectives.

## Why follow this work

Eakin’s research is useful for comparing everyday and crisis cooperation. A promising question concerns which arrangements survive the end of an emergency and why. The research map should record interview coverage, missing perspectives and evidence supporting reported learning. An institutional profile establishes thematic relevance but cannot replace assessment of a particular method. Including the researcher implies neither partnership nor her support for the programme.

**Access and reading limits.** The ASU profile and abstract of Eakin and Shearing’s article were inspected. Full interview materials and other projects were not examined.
