## Direction of work

The GovTech team publishes comparative information on digital government, including citizen participation. Dener’s profile supports his involvement in digital governance. GTMI 2025 is a concrete resource to inspect. It is an institutional indicator collection with documentation rather than experimental evidence of digitalisation’s benefits.

## ORI critical analysis

For voluntary governance, the key distinction is between having a channel and distributing authority. A portal can accept suggestions without requiring anyone to respond. An online complaint form can improve contact while leaving decisions opaque. Describing a digital function should therefore include establishing its place in the actual process.

Accessibility across users is the next question. Formal opportunities mean little when language, technical or administrative barriers intervene. ORI should compare reported availability with the experiences of people who struggle to use a channel. Offline alternatives and opportunities to participate through a representative are particularly relevant.

Comparative indicators help select cases but do not establish causality. Organisations with better digital services may also have greater resources and stronger oversight. Estimating the effect of a new channel requires a separate design and measures of changed decisions, not just submission counts.

## Why follow this work

The team’s resources help locate public documents and frame questions about the transition from infrastructure to influence. ORI’s next check could trace a typical citizen proposal through response, decision and appeal. Analysis should record data versions and collection methods. Interest in this resource does not make a ranking a substitute for legitimacy assessment, and mentioning Dener does not attribute every team product to him personally or imply a relationship with ORI.

**Access and reading limits.** The official GTMI 2025 page and Dener’s profile were inspected. His GovTech role is supported by the profile; personal coauthorship of the specific 2025 report was not established. Microdata were not reanalysed.
