## G1 — Ostrom 2009, A general framework for analyzing sustainability of social-ecological systems

Source: https://www.science.org/doi/10.1126/science.1172133

Year: 2009. Reading depth: publisher abstract and original register; ten variables, with no independent case verification.

Limitations: Analytical framework and synthesis, not a single causal test.

## G2 — Kosfeld, Okada & Riedl 2009, Institution Formation in Public Goods Games

Source: https://www.aeaweb.org/articles?id=10.1257/aer.99.4.1335

Year: 2009. Reading depth: publisher abstract; full text not studied.

Limitations: Laboratory game and model; transfer to real institutions has not been established.

## G3 — Olken 2007, Monitoring Corruption: Evidence from a Field Experiment in Indonesia

Source: https://www.journals.uchicago.edu/doi/10.1086/517935

Year: 2007. Reading depth: publisher abstract; method and result in the author's summary.

Limitations: Government audit of rural roads; missing expenditure is estimated indirectly.

## G4 — Mansuri & Rao 2013, Localizing Development: Does Participation Work?

Source: https://openknowledge.worldbank.org/entities/publication/7ec97d6d-602c-5525-858a-0bfc6cafff7f

Year: 2013. Reading depth: publisher description and summary; chapters and all primary studies were not checked.

Limitations: Institutional review of heterogeneous projects, not a single causal estimate.

## G5 — UNFCCC 2023, Outcome of the first global stocktake

Source: https://unfccc.int/sites/default/files/resource/cma2023_16a01_adv_.pdf

Year: 2023. Reading depth: official decision; selected relevant paragraphs from the original register.

Limitations: Interstate process, not voluntary local self-governance and not a causal test.

## G6 — Poteete & Ostrom 2004, Heterogeneity, Group Size and Collective Action

Source: https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1111/j.1467-7660.2004.00360.x

Year: 2004. Reading depth: publisher abstract and bibliographic record.

Limitations: Forest communities; review interpretation of heterogeneity and size without a universal effect.

## G7 — Ostrom 2007, A diagnostic approach for going beyond panaceas

Source: https://www.pnas.org/doi/10.1073/pnas.0702288104

Year: 2007. Reading depth: publisher abstract and PubMed.

Limitations: Theoretical/diagnostic article, not a test of a specific rule.

## G8 — Ostrom 2010, Polycentric systems for coping with collective action and global environmental change

Source: https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S0959378010000634

Year: 2010. Reading depth: publisher abstract.

Limitations: Argument for multilevel climate governance, not causal evidence of superiority.

## G9 — Cox, Arnold & Villamayor Tomás 2010, A Review of Design Principles for Community-based Natural Resource Management

Source: https://ecologyandsociety.org/vol15/iss4/art38/

Year: 2010. Reading depth: issue page and abstract of the original review.

Limitations: Review of a sample of cases and principles; success measures vary and selection risk remains.

## G10 — Power to the People: Evidence from a Randomized Field Experiment on Community-Based Monitoring in Uganda

Source: https://academic.oup.com/qje/article-abstract/124/2/735/1905094

Year: 2009. Reading depth: publisher abstract.

Limitations: Field experiment in Ugandan public clinics; intervention package and context matter.

## G11 — Casey, Glennerster & Miguel 2012, Reshaping Institutions: Evidence on Aid Impacts Using a Preanalysis Plan

Source: https://academic.oup.com/qje/article-abstract/127/4/1755/1841616

Year: 2012. Reading depth: publisher abstract and available author manuscript inspected selectively.

Limitations: Randomization of a Sierra Leone program; outcomes outside the program and follow-up horizon are limited.

## G12 — Banerjee et al. 2010, Pitfalls of Participatory Programs

Source: https://www.aeaweb.org/articles?id=10.1257/pol.2.1.1

Year: 2010. Reading depth: abstract and publisher page.

Limitations: Field experiment in Indian education; components and context limit transfer.
