# Voluntary Coordination

[Русский](README.md)

[Research map](RESEARCH_MAP.en.md) · [Карта литературы на русском](RESEARCH_MAP.en.md)

**Central question:** Under what conditions do people support shared rules and accountability?

We study institutions for shared resources, polycentric governance, participation, and auditing. The reviews compare theoretical frameworks, laboratory games, and field studies. Particular attention is paid to the differences between the emergence of cooperation, the fairness of rules, and long-term legitimacy.

## What the sources report

### CIV-01
Some communities organize the governance of shared resources themselves; outcomes depend on the characteristics of the system and its context.

**Limitations:** The authors' framework synthesizes research; it is not one global experiment.

**Sources:** [G1: Ostrom 2009, A general framework for analyzing sustainability of social-ecological systems](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/19628857/)

### CIV-02
In a laboratory, endogenous institution formation increased cooperation, but did not always occur; unfair opportunities to free-ride reduced acceptance of the institution.

**Limitations:** Transfer to large political systems has not been established separately.

**Sources:** [G2: Kosfeld, Okada & Riedl 2009, Institution Formation in Public Goods Games](https://www.aeaweb.org/articles?id=10.1257/aer.99.4.1335)

### CIV-03
In an experiment on rural road construction in Indonesia, increasing the probability of an audit reduced unexplained expenditure.

**Limitations:** The effect applies to a specific implementation and setting.

**Sources:** [G3: Olken 2007, Monitoring Corruption: Evidence from a Field Experiment in Indonesia](https://www.journals.uchicago.edu/doi/10.1086/517935)

### CIV-04
The World Bank authors' synthesis highlights context, elite capture, and weak monitoring across many organized-participation projects.

**Limitations:** An institutional review is not a single causal estimate.

**Sources:** [G4: Mansuri & Rao 2013, Localizing Development: Does Participation Work?](https://www.worldbank.org/en/research/publication/localizing-development-does-participation-work)

### CIV-05
The first global stocktake under the Paris Agreement noted near-universal climate action while also finding that overall progress toward the goals was insufficient.

**Limitations:** This is not a causal estimate comparing voluntary governance with the absence of an agreement.

**Sources:** [G5: UNFCCC decision 1/CMA.5, Outcome of the first global stocktake](https://unfccc.int/sites/default/files/resource/cma2023_16a01_adv_.pdf)

## How to read this review

A claim made by a source does not mean its result was independently replicated. Philosophical arguments, correlations, and causal experiments have different evidential status. Reading depth and limitations are listed in the [source register](../../data/sources.json).
