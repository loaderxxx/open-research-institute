# Evgeny Z. Kvon — distal gene regulation and Range Extender

[Русский](researcher-03.md)

**Access.** Checked 25 October 2026: UC Irvine institutional news and indexed abstract and results from Bower et al. (Nature, 2025). Supplements and the laboratory's complete publication corpus were not examined.

Kvon's team investigates distal enhancer activity. In work with Grace Bower and colleagues, regulatory-element replacements in mice demonstrate a role for REX in long-range enhancer action. REX itself lacks conventional enhancer activity in the reported test; adding it enables other elements to act across greater distances. This is a specific mechanism in the studied limb-development context.

**ORI critical analysis.** The most useful distinction is between an element specifying tissue activity and enabling action far from a promoter. If these properties can be separated experimentally, an activity test beside a reporter cannot answer every question about native function. ORI should therefore retain construct design and distance alongside the result rather than using an unconditional label such as “active enhancer”.

Transplanting an element also changes several environmental properties. Evaluation should ask whether comparisons distinguish distance from promoter compatibility, chromatin accessibility and neighbouring regulatory sequences. Necessity at one position and sufficiency at another are strong but contextual statements. They do not establish that one element explains every long-range interaction in the genome.

The group is worth following because it combines sequence analysis with tests of organismal development. A useful next ORI step is to compare elements according to properties established by reporter assays, endogenous intervention and phenotype observation. Such a table would help evaluate computational enhancer–gene maps and identify where functional context is missing. The institutional news provides an entry point to the study, but its accessible account cannot substitute for experimental evidence. Mentioning the researcher implies no partnership, support for ORI or independent assessment of our materials by the laboratory. The relevant lesson concerns how to specify the range and context of a regulatory claim.
