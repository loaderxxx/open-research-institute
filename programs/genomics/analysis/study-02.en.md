# Xie et al. (2025) — DNA-guided transcription factor interactions extend human gene regulatory code

[Русский](study-02.md)

**Access.** Checked 25 October 2026: indexed abstract and selected main-text sections of the Nature paper. The page notes a correction dated 3 June 2025; its contents were not separately checked. Supplements were not read.

The study uses CAP-SELEX to investigate interactions between DNA-bound transcription factors. The authors report 2,198 interacting pairs and show that joint preferences can depend on motif spacing and orientation or differ from individual-factor motifs. It extends regulatory descriptions from independent binding sites to contextual recognition by interacting factors.

**ORI critical analysis.** For cell identity, the important distinction is that two motifs do not by themselves determine the consequence of joint action. Yet biochemical interaction potential does not establish that the proteins coexist at appropriate concentrations and states in a particular cell. Connecting the result to expression requires additional steps through site accessibility, factor availability and functional output.

A large interaction map helps constrain hypotheses, but its incompleteness may be systematic. An undetected pair may depend on measurement conditions or another participant; a detected pair may be irrelevant in the selected tissue. Reading should distinguish missing signal, a negative control and evidence that interaction is absent. Similarly, a sequence-predicted motif does not establish observed occupancy by a protein.

An appropriate ORI next step is to select several pairs relevant to one cellular context and compare biochemical findings with independent binding and perturbation evidence. Such a table should identify which links were measured directly. “Regulatory code” names a research problem here; it does not imply a complete dictionary translating DNA unambiguously into cellular function. Structural and genomic support strengthen particular conclusions without replacing evaluation for every new pair, tissue and developmental stage. This entry consequently treats the study as evidence for combinatorial specificity rather than a universal solved model of regulation, and it leaves the complete correction and supplementary record for subsequent close reading.
