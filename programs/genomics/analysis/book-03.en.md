# Maxine Singer and Paul Berg — Genes & Genomes (1991)

[Русский](book-03.md)

**Access and status.** Checked 24 October 2026: MIT Press bibliographic information and short description. Chapter content was not obtained. This is an initial critical assessment of the book's role; a substantial dossier requires the full text.

The publisher describes an account of molecular structures and mechanisms underlying genetic-information use in complex organisms, emphasising experimental principles and methods. Publication dates to 1991. For ORI, its principal value is a historical comparison between mechanistic molecular genetics and contemporary genome-scale measurement.

**ORI critical analysis.** A substantive reading question is not how well the book anticipated newer technologies, but how evidence was constructed with smaller datasets. Selective intervention, outcome measurement and alternative explanations matter regardless of dataset size. A modern model may rank millions of variants, yet prediction volume does not remove the need to justify what each measured association means. Historical comparison can therefore separate research scale from the strength of causal inference.

At the same time, the book's age limits its use as a reference for current genomic resources. Earlier experimental capabilities, terminology or boundaries of knowledge should not be transferred to present research without checking. Reading notes should distinguish durable principles, historical claims and questions now addressed by new kinds of evidence. A record of these differences would be more useful than declaring the entire text either obsolete or permanently correct.

For the programme, one class of regulatory experiment could be compared with a modern variant-to-gene study. The comparison should identify where measurement changes and where the logic of testing necessity or sufficiency persists. The publisher abstract cannot support attribution of particular examples, chapters or conclusions to the book. This entry therefore does not reconstruct its contents or substitute for reading. A lawful copy and explicit identification of inspected sections are necessary before the historical perspective can become a detailed source analysis. The current contribution is a bounded critical framework for that work.
