# Kagda et al. (2025) — Data navigation on the ENCODE portal

[Русский](study-01.md)

**Access.** Checked 25 October 2026: abstract, introduction and sections on data categories, processing and search in the open Nature Communications article. Experiment files were not downloaded. This describes data infrastructure rather than a single biological experiment.

The paper explains ENCODE's organisation, distinguishing functional-genomics experiments, functional element characterisation, single-cell data and computational annotations. It describes metadata, uniform pipelines and file-selection tools. Some experiments nevertheless contain laboratory-processed results, others uniformly processed results, and some contain both.

**ORI critical analysis.** The main methodological implication is that citing ENCODE does not adequately describe a dataset. A comparison must identify samples, assay, genome assembly, processing version and inclusion rules. Otherwise, an apparent cellular-state difference may instead reflect laboratory or project-phase differences. A common portal makes discovery easier without turning its contents into one coordinated experiment.

Measured signals, predicted annotations and intervention results must be distinguished. Displaying them together on genomic coordinates is useful for hypotheses but does not give every track equal evidential status. Coincident accessibility and transcription may support a regulatory association without establishing that the element is necessary for the particular gene. Functional evidence should retain its cellular context and intervention type.

A reasonable initial ORI output is a small dataset passport containing identifiers, controls and technical limitations. Coordinate consistency and file provenance can then be checked before complex modelling. Rich metadata do not eliminate incomplete tissue and state coverage; absence of an appropriate record cannot be interpreted as biological absence. The article provides guidance for selecting evidence, not proof that every catalogue element is functional. This assessment does not re-evaluate the quality of the entire portal. Its practical contribution is to make source selection reproducible enough that later conclusions can be traced back to specific measurements instead of to the consortium name alone.

**Sources:** [Nature Communications article](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-025-64343-9); [portal described in the article](https://www.encodeproject.org/data/).
