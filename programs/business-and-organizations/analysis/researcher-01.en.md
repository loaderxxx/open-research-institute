## Research question and exemplar

The institutional profile describes Brynjolfsson’s research on information technology productivity and the role of organisational capital. He directs the Stanford Digital Economy Lab. A concrete programme example is Generative AI at Work, coauthored with Danielle Li and Lindsey Raymond, which analyses staggered assistant deployment in customer support. It illustrates using workplace data to examine technology within an organisational setting.

## ORI critical analysis

This line matters because model capability and economic performance do not automatically coincide. Task allocation, training, usage rules and answer verification intervene between them. Studies of particular deployments can make those intermediate links observable. A technological effect in one company, however, does not establish an aggregate outcome for an industry or economy.

The programme should follow designs and measurement units rather than relying on an author’s general reputation. Output per hour, customer satisfaction, employment changes and income distribution answer different questions. Publications should be compared by counterfactual, observation period and organisational changes accompanying adoption. Propositions about the future of work require broader support than a single successful workflow.

## Why follow this work

ORI should watch for longer observations of skills, repeated deployments in other organisations and measures of complementary expenditure. A particularly useful question is which organisational additions turn individual assistance into joint output. An institutional profile helps navigate sources; it does not validate every conclusion. No partnership with the researcher or laboratory is asserted here. This entry selects a research direction for reading rather than offering a complete assessment of the scholar’s work or a forecast of returns to technology.

**Access and reading limits.** The Stanford Economics profile was checked; the GSB record did not open directly. Selected sections of Generative AI at Work supplied the exemplar, not the researcher’s complete body of work.
