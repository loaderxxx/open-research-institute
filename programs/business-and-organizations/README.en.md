## Scope

The programme concerns entrepreneurship, company structure and interactions between people within organisations. Its focus is the distribution of authority, knowledge sharing, team coordination and decision-making. A separate topic is AI adoption: which tasks are delegated, how workflows change and how the quality of decisions is evaluated. The programme studies organisational mechanisms; investment recommendations are outside its scope.

## Priority questions

1. **Entrepreneurship and company structure.** How are entrepreneurial ideas developed and tested? What conditions affect the choice between an internal team, a partnership and an external provider? How does the distribution of decisions change as a company grows?
2. **Incentives and coordination.** How do goals, rewards and access to information affect cooperation? When do metrics support work, and when do they encourage actions that diverge from an organisation's objectives? How should the interests of employees and customers enter the assessment of outcomes?
3. **Decisions and AI adoption.** Which tasks and powers are delegated to AI? How can quality, time and costs be compared before and after adoption? How can decisions remain open to checking and correction?

## Next stage: collecting information

1. Clarify definitions, units of analysis and the scope of the first review: entrepreneur, team, company and individual workflow.
2. Collect scholarly reviews and primary studies of entrepreneurship, organisational structure, incentives and AI adoption. Record search queries, selection criteria and gaps in coverage.
3. Extract each study's context, methods, measures, findings and limitations. Distinguish theoretical explanations, observational associations and estimates of causal effects.
4. Compare the evidence across the three priorities and choose a focused question for the first review. Consider separately whether findings transfer across industries, countries and company sizes.

## Expected materials

- A map of questions and an annotated source list recording reading depth.
- An initial review of entrepreneurship, company structure and coordination, with a table of comparable studies.
- A separate account of AI adoption assessment: tasks, quality measures, organisational changes and open questions.

These are planned materials, not a list of completed studies.
