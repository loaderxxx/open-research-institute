## Research question and exemplar

The UIC profile describes religion as both a belief system and group membership relevant to moral decisions and intergroup relations. Pasek directs the Belief, Identity, and Group Relations Lab. Concrete examples include the multinational trust study and the Fiji experiment examining religious thought when conflict is salient. Their interventions and outcomes are much narrower than religion considered as a whole.

## ORI critical analysis

This line helps separate individual representations from social categorisation. People may invoke a universal moral demand while belonging to a group with its own boundaries. Those mechanisms can produce different predictions. A single religiosity measure can therefore conceal several processes with different consequences.

Experimental reading should identify what instructions change: thought accessibility, perspective-taking, expected observation or compliance with perceived researcher expectations. These alternatives require different tests. Preregistration and multiple cultural settings improve research discipline without eliminating uncertainty about whether game outcomes represent everyday cooperation.

## Why follow this work

ORI should track exact interventions, public protocols and subsequent tests of persistence. Studies comparing expectations of another group with that group’s actual behaviour are particularly useful. Such comparisons distinguish behavioural change from correction of mistaken beliefs about partners.

Naming the laboratory implies neither collaboration nor endorsement of ORI. An institutional profile provides navigation rather than evidence for a result. New studies should be assessed through their designs and inference boundaries, including null findings and cases where anticipated expansion of cooperation does not appear. Following this research means tracing a specific explanatory programme while retaining room for competing mechanisms and evidence.

**Access and reading limits.** The UIC profile and abstracts of two coauthored experimental papers were inspected. The laboratory’s complete publication record was not reviewed.
