## Question, method and result

In Jerusalem in July 2019, fundraisers alternated religious and secular attire while collecting for a neutral charity. The 2025 article reports more frequent donations among religious residents and preference for religious fundraisers. Shared Jewish affiliation did not eliminate a boundary associated with religiosity level. This is a field study of a particular encounter, not measurement of all assistance participants provide.

## ORI critical analysis

The researcher-manipulated signal must be separated from residents’ observed characteristics. Fundraiser attire varies experimentally; donor religiosity is not randomly assigned. Comparing religious and secular residents therefore does not itself establish that belief causes generosity. Income, being home during collection hours and other differences can affect observed behaviour.

A donation to a neutral organisation also involves trust in an intermediary. Responses to clothing might reflect expected honesty, perceived similarity or familiar social rules. Separating these mechanisms would require additional measures that should not be attributed to the study without checking. Ingroup preference and hostility towards outsiders are distinct outcomes.

## Programme relevance

For ORI, the study shows why religious identity cannot be coded only by denomination. The encounter, membership signal, beneficiary and possibility of refusal matter. Comparisons with monetary games should consider actual costs and the visibility of interaction. Further reading should examine residents who did and did not open their doors, religiosity coding and robustness across neighbourhoods. One urban experiment cannot rank religions or determine the everyday morality of all believers. Its contribution is a bounded behavioural comparison that makes the definition of the relevant group more precise.

**Access and reading limits.** The abstract and portions of introduction, procedure and discussion of the open article were inspected. Not all tables or supplements were checked.
