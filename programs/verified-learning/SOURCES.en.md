# Sources and Reading Depth

[Русский](SOURCES.md)

This register covers the in-depth reviews in this program. Checked: 23 September 2026. Access to an abstract does not mean the full article was read.

## S03 — SWE-Bench+: Enhanced Coding Benchmark for LLMs

Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.06992

Year: 2024. Reading depth: abstract and HTML: results and conclusion.

Limitations: Estimates from an audit of successful patches in a specific system; not a universal error rate for any benchmark.

## S04 — Judging the Judges: A Systematic Study of Position Bias in LLM-as-a-Judge

Source: https://arxiv.org/html/2406.07791v9

Year: 2025. Reading depth: v9 abstract and HTML; previously read details from an earlier version were used only for the general qualitative formulation.

Limitations: Bias depends on model, task, and difference in answer quality; it does not measure every kind of unverifiable claim.

## S06 — Training Verifiers to Solve Math Word Problems

Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2110.14168

Year: 2021. Reading depth: abstract and HTML: training setup and ablations.

Limitations: Arithmetic problems with known answers; the original procedure required a large number of samples.

## S07 — Let's Verify Step by Step

Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2305.20050

Year: 2023. Reading depth: abstract and HTML; key results.

Limitations: MATH and costly expert annotations of steps; the result cannot be transferred to arbitrary work episodes.

## S08 — Distilling Step-by-Step! Outperforming Larger Language Models with Less Training Data and Smaller Model Sizes

Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2305.02301

Year: 2023. Reading depth: abstract and HTML: method overview.

Limitations: Four NLP benchmarks; useful training signals do not prove that every explanation is true.

## S09 — STaR: Bootstrapping Reasoning With Reasoning

Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2203.14465

Year: 2022. Reading depth: abstract; iteration scheme.

Limitations: A correct final answer does not confirm that the rationale is correct; a known answer is required.

## S10 — AI models collapse when trained on recursively generated data

Source: https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-024-07566-y

Year: 2024. Reading depth: abstract and HTML: methods and limitations of the recursive model.

Limitations: Risk under recursive replacement/contamination, not a prohibition on all synthetic data.

## S11 — Is Model Collapse Inevitable? Breaking the Curse of Recursion by Accumulating Real and Synthetic Data

Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2404.01413

Year: 2024. Reading depth: abstract.

Limitations: Retaining original data prevented a specific collapse metric; it does not guarantee that every new episode is correct.

## S12 — Textbooks Are All You Need

Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2306.11644

Year: 2023. Reading depth: abstract.

Limitations: Substantial training resources and a mixture of selected web data and synthetic data; coding-test results are not a general recipe.

## VL13 — Math-Shepherd: Verify and Reinforce LLMs Step-by-step without Human Annotations

Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2312.08935

Year: 2023. Reading depth: abstract and v3 bibliographic page.

Limitations: Automatic step labels are inferred from later outcomes; this is not independent human verification and was studied on mathematics.

## VL14 — Large Language Models Cannot Self-Correct Reasoning Yet

Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2310.01798

Year: 2023. Reading depth: abstract and bibliographic page.

Limitations: Examines internal self-correction without external feedback; the conclusion does not deny the usefulness of verifiable tools.

## VL15 — Self-Consistency Improves Chain of Thought Reasoning in Language Models

Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2203.11171

Year: 2022. Reading depth: abstract and search record for the primary article.

Limitations: Agreement among samples improves accuracy on studied tasks, but does not prove that an agreed answer is true.

## VL16 — Collapse or Thrive? Perils and Promises of Synthetic Data in a Self-Generating World

Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.16713

Year: 2024. Reading depth: abstract and bibliographic page.

Limitations: Three model settings; the limited-volume regime shows gradual degradation and does not generalize to all corpora.

## VL17 — Author Correction: AI models collapse when trained on recursively generated data

Source: https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-025-08905-3

Year: 2025. Reading depth: full text of the brief correction notice.

Limitations: Corrects the symbols α, β, γ in the theoretical intuition section; it is not a retraction.

## VL18 — LLMs cannot find reasoning errors, but can correct them when given the error location

Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2311.08516

Year: 2023. Reading depth: abstract in the primary publication results.

Limitations: The logical-error localization setup is limited to the models and tasks studied.
