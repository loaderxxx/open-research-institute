## C1 — Identifying indicators of consciousness in AI systems

Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41219038/

Year: 2025. Reading depth: PubMed abstract and metadata; not the full text

Limitations: Theoretical and methodological article; not an experiment or a calibration of machine experience.

## C2 — Adversarial testing of global neuronal workspace and integrated information theories of consciousness

Source: https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-025-08888-1

Year: 2025. Reading depth: Open main text: introduction, methods, results, and discussion; supplements sampled according to the previous registry

Limitations: Total sample of 256; above-threshold human vision; specific analyses and mathematical cores of the theories are not identical to the entire experiment.

## C3 — A theoretically based index of consciousness independent of sensory processing and behavior

Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/23946194/

Year: 2013. Reading depth: PubMed abstract and indexed author finding

Limitations: PCI was tested on human states; it is neither an exact formal quantity of IIT nor a validated measure for AI.

## C4 — No-Report Paradigms: Extracting the True Neural Correlates of Consciousness

Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/26585549/

Year: 2015. Reading depth: PubMed abstract and publisher's abstract

Limitations: A methodological argument about the report confound; a no-report measure itself still requires validation.

## C5 — Gamma band activity and the P3 reflect post-perceptual processes, not visual awareness

Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4169212/

Year: 2014. Reading depth: PubMed abstract and available excerpts of the open full text

Limitations: A visual task; does not prove the absence of every frontal role in all forms of consciousness.

## C6 — Distilling the neural correlates of consciousness

Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/22192881/

Year: 2012. Reading depth: PubMed abstract

Limitations: A methodological review of assumptions and implications of NCC, not a standalone causal experiment.

## C7 — Conscious Processing and the Global Neuronal Workspace Hypothesis

Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8770991/

Year: 2020. Reading depth: PubMed abstract and selected portions of the available review article's main text

Limitations: An author review of GNWT; specific temporal predictions depend on the model and task.

## C8 — Integrated information theory (IIT) 4.0: Formulating the properties of phenomenal existence in physical terms

Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10581496/

Year: 2023. Reading depth: Abstract and selected sections of the open text

Limitations: An authorial formalism; disputed axioms and difficulty transferring it to real large networks.

## C9 — A higher-order theory of emotional consciousness

Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5347624/

Year: 2017. Reading depth: Abstract and selected sections of the open text

Limitations: One version of the family of higher-order theories, not a unified position held by all authors.

## C10 — Visual Functions Generating Conscious Seeing

Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/32116908/

Year: 2020. Reading depth: PubMed abstract and selected portions of the open review text

Limitations: An author's interpretation of visual recurrent processing; not a direct test of all theories.

## C11 — Consciousness and cortical reactivity: a within-state study during non-rapid eye movement sleep

Source: https://www.nature.com/articles/srep30932

Year: 2016. Reading depth: Abstract and selected sections of the open text

Limitations: Retrospective report on awakening depends on memory; NREM is not one uniform phenomenal state.

## C12 — Cognitive Motor Dissociation in Disorders of Consciousness

Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39141852/

Year: 2024. Reading depth: PubMed abstract and bibliographic information

Limitations: 60/241 refers to patients examined without external command-following; a negative test does not prove absence of experience.

## C13 — An adversarial collaboration protocol for testing contrasting predictions of global neuronal workspace and integrated information theory

Source: https://journals.plos.org/plosone/article?id=10.1371/journal.pone.0268577

Year: 2023. Reading depth: Abstract and selected main sections of the open protocol

Limitations: A preregistered plan is not results; moving from theory to measurement involves assumptions.

## C14 — GNW theoretical framework and the “adversarial testing of global neuronal workspace and integrated information theories of consciousness”

Source: https://academic.oup.com/nc/article/2025/1/niaf037/8280147

Year: 2025. Reading depth: Main argument according to the previous registry and a search check; the corrected version was not cross-checked

Limitations: An interested response by GNWT proponents, not an independent replication; C15 is a correction whose content is unknown.

Source: https://academic.oup.com/nc/article/2026/1/niag020/8666310

Year: 2026. Reading depth: Metadata and search snippet only; content unavailable

Limitations: The existence of a correction is confirmed, but its subject and impact are unknown; do not attribute specific changes to it.
