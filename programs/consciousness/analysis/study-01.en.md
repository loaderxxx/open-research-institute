# Cogitate — Adversarial testing of GNWT and IIT

[Русская версия](study-01.md)

**Access and reading limits.** Inspected the abstract, accessible main-text passages and data statement. The preregistration and underlying arrays were not separately analysed.

**Research result.** Cogitate compares advance-agreed predictions from global neuronal workspace theory and integrated information theory. The study involved 256 participants, visual stimuli and several recording methods: fMRI, MEG and intracranial electrophysiology. Its findings challenge particular predictions on both sides, including expected spatial and temporal patterns of activity and interaction. It tests specified biological implementations in a defined visual task, not every possible version of an explanation of experience.

**ORI critical analysis.** The main methodological contribution is to fix divergent expectations before observing results. This reduces the freedom to redescribe any pattern retrospectively as support. The strength of an adversarial test nevertheless depends on whether proponents genuinely agreed that the predictions were decisive and shared an understanding of permissible auxiliary assumptions.

Several recording methods provide different observational windows, rather than three entirely independent proofs of one quantity. Their spatial and temporal sensitivities differ; a missing effect can depend on power and analysis choices. A negative result should therefore be read alongside the prespecified criterion and an assessment of the effect the method could detect.

The programme should separately consider perceptual content, availability for reporting, attention and task requirements. A signal accompanying a participant’s decision is not necessarily the mechanism of experience itself. A useful reading output is a matrix connecting theory, exact prediction, measurement, result and permissible revision. This helps distinguish productive theoretical refinement from rescuing an imprecise formulation. The study substantially advances model comparison without establishing a final winner or settling the philosophical question of subjectivity. Its broader contribution is a more accountable way to argue about evidence, with commitments that can be checked against what was actually observed.
