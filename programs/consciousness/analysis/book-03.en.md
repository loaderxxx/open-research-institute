# Christof Koch — The Feeling of Life Itself

[Русская версия](book-03.md)

**Access and reading limits.** Checked the MIT Press description. The book first appeared in 2019; the linked paperback is dated 2020. The complete text was not read.

**Source position.** The publisher describes the book as a defence of an integrated-information approach to consciousness. Koch starts from subjective experience and seeks a quantitative theory linking it with a system’s physical organisation. The subtitle expresses a strong position on the limits of computational explanation. This is an argument for a particular theory, not an evenly balanced survey of alternatives or an already completed validation of every implication of IIT.

**ORI critical analysis.** The programme should examine the transition from proposed properties of experience to claims about physical organisation. Each transition needs to be reconstructed separately: is it a definition, an additional postulate or an empirical hypothesis? Otherwise, agreement with a description of experience can quietly become agreement with a particular mathematical formalism.

A quantity defined within a theory should also be distinguished from a practical measurement. Even a tractable proxy does not automatically become a direct consciousness meter. Its relationship to the theoretical quantity depends on assumptions that should be examined when the scale, system boundary or observational conditions change.

The book is useful alongside experiments testing specific neural predictions, including the Cogitate adversarial study. Such a test should not, without further argument, be described as testing every mathematical and metaphysical commitment of the theory. A sensible ORI output is a dependency table linking postulates, biological implementations and measurable consequences. It would show which components might be revised after new data and which require a different form of argument. This permits discussion of possible experience across different systems without assigning consciousness merely from complexity, connectivity or convincing behaviour. Reading the complete book remains necessary before judging the strength of its detailed defence.
