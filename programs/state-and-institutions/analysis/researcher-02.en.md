## Research question and exemplar

Kraay’s profile lists research on growth, poverty, inequality and institutions. A concrete programme example is the Worldwide Governance Indicators methodology. The 2026 release page cites Daniel Kaufmann and Aart Kraay’s 2024 paper as an account of the original methodology. The 2026 update itself is attributed to the World Bank. The map’s claim about personal authorship of the current release therefore needs qualification and is not repeated here.

## ORI critical analysis

The programme’s main interest is the relationship between governance as a concept and its statistical representation. A composite indicator requires source selection, scale alignment and assumptions about the latent characteristic being measured. Those decisions affect interpretation even when calculations are careful. It is useful to ask not only whether rankings are stable, but which question an indicator can actually answer.

Evaluating a particular reform especially requires avoiding a jump from a national index to a mechanism without intermediate evidence. Changing governance perceptions may accompany increased trust without automatically demonstrating a better appeals process or fewer access barriers. Each such link needs its own investigation.

## Why follow this work

ORI should follow methodological updates, published underlying data and explanations of uncertainty. A material question is which changes in results arise from new information and which from a new measurement model. This research line supports disciplined comparison and limits excessive inference from indices. It does not remove the need to examine citizens’ and organisations’ actual experiences. The profile describes a research direction, implies no partnership with ORI and does not suggest the author agrees with every political interpretation of his indicators.

**Access and reading limits.** The World Bank profile, WGI 2026 page and documentation were inspected. Personal authorship of the 2026 release methodology was not verified; the confirmed Kaufmann–Kraay 2024 paper is identified.
