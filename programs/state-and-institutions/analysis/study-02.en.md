## What the resource contains

GTMI 2025 describes digital government in 197 economies using 48 indicators across four areas: core systems, services, engagement and enabling conditions. It combines self-reports from 158 economies with public information for the remainder. The World Bank explicitly presents it as an overview of practices, not a ranking or performance evaluation. It is diagnostic, not a causal estimate of digitalisation.

## ORI critical analysis

A portal’s existence does not establish successful service delivery. Users may encounter missing documents, difficult identification, inaccessible interfaces or mandatory offline completion. Digital infrastructure measures should therefore be connected to a concrete citizen journey: attempted application, completed procedure and correction of refusal.

Combining self-reporting with public information raises comparability questions. Collection methods may differ in their ability to detect existing capabilities and practical gaps. Before comparing countries, researchers should check the source of each indicator and whether its definition was interpreted consistently. An overall group cannot remove the difference between strong technical infrastructure and weak citizen participation.

## Programme relevance

ORI can use the index as a map of administrative process components. Further analysis should connect a system to authority, funding and the responsible implementing body. Particular attention belongs to groups poorly served by digital channels: people with limited skills, connectivity or nonstandard cases. Before-and-after comparisons should include completed applications, delays and appeals rather than website visits alone. Connecting these observations would turn a diagnostic inventory into a research question while preserving the distinction between technology’s existence, its use and public outcomes. Any causal claim would still require a credible comparison group or another explicit identification strategy.

**Access and reading limits.** The release description, indicator scope and World Bank cautions were inspected. The dashboard and underlying data were not analysed, and the index was not recalculated.
