## Research question and exemplar

Institutional profiles connect Barbera with public finance and citizen–government interaction, Ferry with performance measurement, accountability and field methods, and Hyndman with emeritus accounting scholarship. A concrete joint example is the 2025 introduction Governance and Accountability in Reshaping Public Services. The full author list also includes Mariannunziata Liguori, Sean McCandless and Henry C. Midgley. The three researchers highlighted in the map are not its only authors.

## Content and limits of the example

The abstract connects accountability with legitimacy, power, participation and changing service-delivery arrangements. The article introduces a special issue containing 14 papers. It is a conceptual overview of directions, not a shared experimental evaluation. Each contribution’s method requires separate assessment; a researcher’s interest in field methods does not turn an introductory essay into a field study.

## Critical analysis and relevance for ORI

The programme should distinguish publication of a report from accountability. Information may be available without a person or body able to demand an explanation and secure correction. Where several organisations jointly deliver a service, it is particularly useful to trace who answers to citizens for the final outcome rather than only for an individual contract.

This line merits attention for studying digitalisation and new service arrangements. A practical next step is to choose one accountability chain and compare formal documents with observed complaints, responses and corrections. Analysis should include groups unable to use published reporting easily. Listing these researchers indicates interest in their public work, not partnership with ORI or a claim that they form one permanent team using identical methods.

**Access and reading limits.** Barbera’s and Ferry’s profiles, Queen’s indexed Hyndman record and the introductory article’s abstract were inspected. The special issue’s 14 papers were not fully read.
