# Sources and Reading Depth

[Русский](SOURCES.md)

This register covers the in-depth reviews in this program. Checked: 23 September 2026. Access to an abstract does not mean the full article was read.

## A01 — Why Do Multi-Agent LLM Systems Fail?

Source: https://arxiv.org/html/2503.13657v3

Year: 2025. Reading depth: abstract and selected sections of HTML v3: introduction, taxonomy method, and results.

Limitations: Taxonomy from 150 original traces; 1,642 scale-annotated traces from seven systems include LLM annotation; does not exhaust all failure modes.

## A02 — Improving Multi-Agent Debate with Sparse Communication Topology

Source: https://aclanthology.org/2024.findings-emnlp.427/

Year: 2024. Reading depth: abstract and ACL bibliographic page.

Limitations: Comparable or better results in the tested models and tasks; saving input tokens is not the full cost of research.

## A03 — Enabling Large Language Models to Generate Text with Citations

Source: https://aclanthology.org/2023.emnlp-main.398/

Year: 2023. Reading depth: abstract and ACL bibliographic page; selected PDF excerpt in search results.

Limitations: ALCE evaluates limited sets of questions and documents; correlation between automatic and human ratings does not guarantee performance across all genres.

## A04 — Evaluating Verifiability in Generative Search Engines

Source: https://aclanthology.org/2023.findings-emnlp.467/

Year: 2023. Reading depth: abstract and ACL bibliographic page.

Limitations: Manual audit of four systems and selected queries; not a universal rate for every search engine.

## A05 — SWE-Bench+: Enhanced Coding Benchmark for LLMs

Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.06992

Year: 2024. Reading depth: abstract and bibliographic page; selected details from the 2026-09-22 source register.

Limitations: Audit of selected successful patches from a specific system; the percentage should not be generalized to all coding tasks.

## A06 — Judging the Judges: A Systematic Study of Position Bias in LLM-as-a-Judge

Source: https://arxiv.org/html/2406.07791v9

Year: 2025. Reading depth: abstract of the updated version and selected HTML from the original register.

Limitations: Bias depends on the judge, task, and difference in answer quality; it does not directly measure MAST annotation.

## A07 — Holistic Evaluation of Language Models

Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2211.09110

Year: 2022. Reading depth: abstract and arXiv bibliographic page.

Limitations: Breadth of scenarios does not mean all fields are covered; changing models and metrics limit transfer.

## A08 — Lost in the Middle: How Language Models Use Long Contexts

Source: https://aclanthology.org/2024.tacl-1.9/

Year: 2024. Reading depth: abstract and ACL bibliographic page.

Limitations: Position effect in the studied question-answering and retrieval tasks; not a direct test of scientific review writing.

## A09 — FActScore: Fine-grained Atomic Evaluation of Factual Precision in Long Form Text Generation

Source: https://aclanthology.org/2023.emnlp-main.741/

Year: 2023. Reading depth: abstract and ACL bibliographic page.

Limitations: Atomic factual precision depends on the knowledge base and fact segmentation; it does not measure answer completeness.

## A10 — TruthfulQA: Measuring How Models Mimic Human Falsehoods

Source: https://aclanthology.org/2022.acl-long.229/

Year: 2022. Reading depth: abstract and ACL bibliographic page.

Limitations: Purpose-built questions about common misconceptions; metrics concern historical models.

## A11 — Language Models (Mostly) Know What They Know

Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2207.05221

Year: 2022. Reading depth: abstract and arXiv bibliographic page.

Limitations: Calibration depends on format and task; prediction of knowledge on new tasks is limited.

## A13 — Automatic Evaluation of Attribution by Large Language Models

Source: https://aclanthology.org/2023.findings-emnlp.307/

Year: 2023. Reading depth: abstract and ACL bibliographic page.

Limitations: Curated test across 12 domains and simulated examples; promising results and difficulties do not produce a perfect judge.
