# Huan Sun and OSU NLP — scientific agents

[Русская версия](researcher-01.md)

**Access and reading limits.** Checked the Ohio State profile and ScienceAgentBench abstract. Internal group projects and full experimental materials were not examined.

**Research direction.** Huan Sun’s institutional profile describes work on language models and agents, including their construction, evaluation and internal mechanisms. ScienceAgentBench is a concrete example: scientific tasks become checkable programming outputs with domain-expert involvement. For ORI, the group represents a direction connecting scientific-automation claims with execution of bounded operations rather than dialogue quality alone.

**ORI critical analysis.** This direction is worth following through the quality of task definition. How much real uncertainty does the instruction preserve? Which decisions have the benchmark designers already made? A task that supplies the method and output format tests a different capability from selecting a method for ambiguous data. Comparisons between new studies should retain those distinctions.

A second question concerns the domain expert’s role. An expert might validate task plausibility, inspect code or assess the substantive conclusion; these are different contributions. The fact of expert involvement does not establish which layer of reliability was checked. ORI should track annotation documentation, disagreements and availability of control examples.

A practical monitoring format compares new benchmarks by artefact, checking procedure, cost and ability to localise errors. Particularly useful work makes successful execution independently repeatable and distinguishable from matching a convenient reference. Agent capability should still be described together with its model, tools and execution limits. The group is listed as a source of measurement methods and concrete open tasks, not as a declaration of collaboration, independent reproduction of its results or researcher endorsement of ORI’s interpretation. This preserves the value of a scientific orientation without treating reputation as a substitute for scrutiny.
