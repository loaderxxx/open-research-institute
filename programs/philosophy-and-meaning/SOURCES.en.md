# Sources and Reading Depth

[Русский](SOURCES.md)

This register covers the in-depth reviews in this program. Checked: 23 September 2026. Access to an abstract does not mean the full article was read.

## P1 — Aristotle, Nicomachean Ethics, Book I

Source: https://www.perseus.tufts.edu/hopper/text?doc=Perseus%3Atext%3A1999.01.0054%3Abook%3D1

Year: -340. Reading depth: Book I according to the original register; the Perseus link was unavailable on repeat access.

Limitations: Teleology and a limited conception of citizenship; translation disputes around *eudaimonia*.

## P2 — Dhammapada, verses 1–20, translation Bhikkhu Sujato

Source: https://suttacentral.net/dhp1-20/en/sujato

Year: -300. Reading depth: register and translator's page; web extraction of the text was limited.

Limitations: One verse does not represent the diversity of Buddhist traditions.

## P3 — Susan Wolf, Meaning in Life and Why It Matters

Source: https://personal.lse.ac.uk/ROBERT49/teaching/ph103/pdf/WolfSusanne_2010_Meaning-in-Life-and-Why-It-Matters.pdf

Year: 2010. Reading depth: PDF: introduction, chapters, and commentary section inspected selectively.

Limitations: Objective value and criteria for engagement are disputed.

## P4 — Thomas Nagel, The Absurd

Source: https://philosophy.as.uky.edu/sites/default/files/The%20Absurd%20-%20Thomas%20Nagel.pdf

Year: 1971. Reading depth: full PDF; philosophical argument.

Limitations: Not clinical evidence about the feeling that life is meaningless.

## P5 — John Rawls, The Idea of Public Reason Revisited

Source: https://chicagounbound.uchicago.edu/uclrev/vol64/iss3/1/

Year: 1997. Reading depth: primary-article page, abstract, and bibliography.

Limitations: The procedure of public reason does not eliminate disagreement and is not universal.

## P6 — Amartya Sen, Democracy as a Universal Value

Source: https://www.journalofdemocracy.org/articles/democracy-as-a-universal-value/

Year: 1999. Reading depth: original article page and selected main text.

Limitations: A normative defense of democracy, not proof of a single conception of meaning.

## P7 — Steger et al., The Meaning in Life Questionnaire

Source: https://experts.umn.edu/en/publications/the-meaning-in-life-questionnaire-assessing-the-presence-of-and-s/

Year: 2006. Reading depth: publisher record, abstract, and methodological review.

Limitations: Self-report, samples, and factor model do not establish objective value.

## P8 — Robert Nozick, The Experience Machine

Source: https://rintintin.colorado.edu/~vancecd/phil3160/Nozick1.pdf

Year: 1974. Reading depth: primary excerpt from the book in an academic copy.

Limitations: The thought experiment depends on intuitions and wording.

## P9 — Frank Martela and Michael F. Steger, The three meanings of meaning in life

Source: https://www.researchgate.net/publication/292190023_The_three_meanings_of_meaning_in_life_Distinguishing_coherence_purpose_and_significance

Year: 2016. Reading depth: author's publication abstract.

Limitations: Theoretical distinction among aspects does not prove their cultural invariance.

## P10 — Laura A. King et al., Positive affect and the experience of meaning in life

Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/16448317/

Year: 2006. Reading depth: bibliographic record and abstract of the original study.

Limitations: Experimental and correlational links involving experience do not resolve the normative question.

## P11 — Harry Frankfurt, The Importance of What We Care About

Source: https://www.cambridge.org/core/books/importance-of-what-we-care-about/9691C950461B737990D6CE2AE5967E0A

Year: 1988. Reading depth: publisher record for the collection; general propositions only.

Limitations: Full chapter text was not extracted; details are not attributed to it.
