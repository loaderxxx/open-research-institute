# Digital Continuity of the Person

[Русский](README.md)

**Main question:** What do data about a person preserve, and where is the boundary between a model and a person?

This program brings together the philosophy of personal identity, memory research, connectomics, and behavioral models. We examine what information neural maps provide, which properties must be distinguished from connection structure, and what accurate behavioral prediction shows. Biological preservation, psychological continuity, and continuation of subjective experience are discussed as distinct questions.

## What the sources report

### I-01
There is a complete map of the adult fruit fly brain, a cubic-millimeter reconstruction of human cortex, and a combined structure/activity dataset from a region of mouse cortex; none is a map of the entire functioning person.

**Limitations:** Each dataset substantially expands measurement and modeling capabilities.

**Sources:** [I1: Neuronal wiring diagram of an adult brain](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-024-07558-y); [I2: A petavoxel fragment of human cerebral cortex reconstructed at nanoscale resolution](https://www.science.org/doi/10.1126/science.adk4858); [I3: Functional connectomics spanning multiple areas of mouse visual cortex](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-025-08790-w)

### I-02
In a model of the fly visual system, the connectome constrains the model, but unknown properties had to be optimized for the task; connectivity alone is insufficient to determine a unique dynamics.

**Limitations:** Sufficiency may change at another scale or with additional physiology.

**Sources:** [I4: Connectome-constrained networks predict neural activity across the fly visual system](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-024-07939-3)

### I-03
Activation of labeled mouse hippocampal cells elicited a learned freezing response.

**Limitations:** The response is not equivalent to a complete memory or a person.

**Sources:** [I5: Optogenetic stimulation of a hippocampal engram activates fear memory recall](https://www.nature.com/articles/nature11028)

### I-05
Branching psychological continuation is a reasoned theory of identity, not a measurement of first-person transfer.

**Limitations:** Theories of personal identity that reject branching, or that are biological, compete with it.

**Sources:** [I7: Uploading and Branching Identity](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s11023-014-9352-8)
