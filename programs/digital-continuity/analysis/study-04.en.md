## Subject

The authors review virtual brain twins as personalised computational models integrating structural and functional data. Network nodes represent aggregate neural activity, with connections specifying interactions between regions. EBRAINS explains three stages: structural mapping, dynamic-model integration and individual fitting. The journal issue is dated 2026, while the DOI and early publication date belong to 2025.

## Main significance

Our interpretation is that the word twin is informative only when accompanied by a list of modelled quantities. A model can predict specified dynamics or compare intervention scenarios without describing every memory. Personalisation means that parameters depend on individual measurements; it does not establish the presence of that person inside the software.

## What requires testing

A review combines methods and potential uses, but cannot replace independent evaluation of each application. Agreement with fitting data does not establish predictive performance under new conditions. Assessment should ask whether measurements uniquely identify parameters, how sensitive results are to prior assumptions and whether alternative models produce similar answers. Clinical tables and computations were not reproduced in this entry.

## ORI application

We propose describing any future twin through input measurements, modelling assumptions, predicted outcomes and applicability limits. Each prediction needs a separate set of held-out observations. This specification would enable comparisons without confusing neurophysiological modelling with a philosophical claim about subjective continuation. It is an ORI research framework, not treatment advice or an already validated programme result.
