## Research direction

Lichtman's official profile connects the laboratory's agenda to how experience becomes reflected in neural-circuit structure. Its work includes connection development and rearrangement, observation of living peripheral circuits, Brainbow fluorescent labelling and serial electron microscopy. Biological questions about learning are therefore linked to instrument development, rather than simply producing increasingly large images.

## Example work

The laboratory publication list includes the human cortical-fragment reconstruction and SmartEM, a machine-learning-guided electron-microscopy method published online in 2025 and in a 2026 issue. The profile and publication list were read; a complete technical assessment of SmartEM was not performed for this entry. Unverified speed or accuracy multipliers are therefore not reported.

## ORI interpretation

A discussion of preserving information about a person first needs to establish what a measurement method can capture. Faster acquisition and reconstruction may enable new observations without proving that those observations suffice to recover memory. Measurement quality, network annotation and a later functional model should be evaluated separately.
