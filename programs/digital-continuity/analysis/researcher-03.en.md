## Research direction

The official Virtual Brain Twin team page lists Viktor Jirsa as scientific coordinator. The programme connects structural and functional brain measurements with network dynamics. In the EBRAINS explanation, individual parameters are fitted to observations and the resulting model explores possible scenarios. This is a particular scientific and engineering objective whose boundaries should be read literally.

## Meaning of personalisation

Our interpretation is that individual fitting concerns correspondence with particular measurements, rather than complete equivalence to a person. Anatomy, dynamic parameters, observed responses and autobiographical information should be distinguished. Possessing one type of individual data does not establish recovery of the others or of subjective experience.

## Research value

The work illustrates a transition from an average description towards a testable prediction for an individual system. Evaluation should identify directly measured parameters, statistically inferred parameters and assumptions supplied without individual measurements. Testing conditions excluded from fitting is especially important. This makes personalisation a substantive research question without promising a complete digital person.

## Limits of this entry

The official team page and approach explanation were read; clinical trials, individual datasets and computations were not examined in full. Potential applications do not establish effectiveness of every procedure. A useful next step is one publication with a defined prediction, examining its validation protocol, alternatives and uncertainty. No ORI affiliation, treatment recommendation, partnership or demonstrated consciousness transfer is implied.
