## I1 — Dorkenwald et al., Neuronal wiring diagram of an adult brain

Source: https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-024-07558-y

Year: 2024. Reading depth: Abstract, main results, and excerpts from the publisher's text

Limitations: Complete anatomy of the adult fruit fly brain; not live dynamics and not a human brain.

## I2 — Shapson-Coe et al., A petavoxel fragment of human cerebral cortex reconstructed at nanoscale resolution

Source: https://www.science.org/doi/10.1126/science.adk4858

Year: 2024. Reading depth: Publisher abstract and indexed finding

Limitations: About 1 mm³ of fixed human tissue; full paper and supplements were not reviewed.

## I3 — MICrONS Consortium, Functional connectomics spanning multiple areas of mouse visual cortex

Source: https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-025-08790-w

Year: 2025. Reading depth: Abstract and indexed excerpts of the methods

Limitations: A local volume of mouse visual cortex; registered and reconstructed cells are not one and the same population.

## I4 — Lappalainen et al., Connectome-constrained networks predict neural activity across the fly visual system

Source: https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-024-07939-3

Year: 2024. Reading depth: Abstract and main text on model assumptions

Limitations: Parameter optimization and a separate visual circuit; the connectome alone does not determine unique dynamics.

## I5 — Liu et al., Optogenetic stimulation of a hippocampal engram activates fear memory recall

Source: https://www.nature.com/articles/nature11028

Year: 2012. Reading depth: Abstract and experimental summary

Limitations: Mouse freezing in a fear-conditioning setting, not restoration of a complete biography.

## I7 — Cerullo, Uploading and Branching Identity

Source: https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s11023-014-9352-8

Year: 2015. Reading depth: Abstract and excerpts of the argument

Limitations: Philosophical argument about conceivable branching, not experimental uploading.

## I8 — Shiu et al., A Drosophila computational brain model reveals sensorimotor processing

Source: https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-024-07763-9

Year: 2024. Reading depth: Abstract and indexed excerpts

Limitations: A simplified spiking model of the fruit fly brain and selected sensorimotor tasks.

## I9 — Ramirez et al., Creating a false memory in the hippocampus

Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/23888038/

Year: 2013. Reading depth: Author abstract in PubMed

Limitations: Contextual learning and a mouse freezing response, not verbal memory content.

## I10 — Roy et al., Brain-wide mapping reveals that engrams for a single memory are distributed across multiple brain regions

Source: https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-022-29384-4

Year: 2022. Reading depth: Abstract and excerpts from the main text

Limitations: A partial map of ensembles for one contextual memory in a mouse using selected markers.

## I11 — Scoville and Milner, Loss of recent memory after bilateral hippocampal lesions

Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/13406589/

Year: 1957. Reading depth: Bibliographic record of the original and abstract of the 2000 reprint, PMID 10678523

Limitations: Nine heterogeneous clinical cases and historical surgical extents.

## I12 — Binz et al., A foundation model to predict and capture human cognition

Source: https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-025-09215-4

Year: 2025. Reading depth: Abstract, introduction, and excerpts of methods/discussion from the publisher's open page

Limitations: Psych-101: 160 experiments and over 10 million choices from many people; not individual consciousness.

## I13 — Namazova et al., Not Yet AlphaFold for the Mind: Evaluating Centaur as a Synthetic Participant

Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2508.07887

Year: 2025. Reading depth: Abstract and indexed excerpts of the preprint

Limitations: Preprint; evaluation of generative distributions under specified conditions does not refute all predictions.

## I14 — Wang et al., Foundation model of neural activity predicts response to new stimulus types

Source: https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-025-08829-y

Year: 2025. Reading depth: Abstract and excerpts of the publisher's discussion; an author correction from 2026 was found, but its contents were not checked

Limitations: Dynamic visual responses in mouse cortex; not a whole brain and not a person. Correction: https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-026-10457-z

## I15 — Locke, An Essay Concerning Human Understanding, II.xxvii, Of Identity and Diversity

Source: https://www.gutenberg.org/files/10615/10615-h/10615-h.htm

Year: 1694. Reading depth: Chapter II.xxvii in the available English text; no philological comparison of editions

Limitations: A primary philosophical text, not neuroscientific evidence.

## I16 — Eric T. Olson, The Human Animal: Personal Identity Without Psychology

Source: https://academic.oup.com/book/3191/chapter/144092799

Year: 1997. Reading depth: Table of contents, publisher abstract, and available excerpts of chapter 6; the full book was not read

Limitations: A primary philosophical argument in the chapter The Biological Approach; not an empirical experiment. The publisher's edition is also dated 1999.
