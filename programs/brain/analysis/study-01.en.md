# Dorkenwald et al. — Neuronal wiring diagram of an adult brain

[Русская версия](study-01.md)

**Access and reading limits.** Inspected the abstract and opening of the open article. The dataset and API were not independently run.

**Research result.** Dorkenwald and colleagues reconstruct the connections of one adult female Drosophila brain, including 139,255 neurons and tens of millions of chemical synapses. This is a spatial reconstruction from electron microscopy, not a recording of the living brain’s activity or a direct measurement of its computations.

**ORI critical analysis.** Its central contribution to the programme is the possibility of specifying circuit hypotheses precisely. Instead of saying that a region “participates in memory,” a researcher can identify cells, connections and alternative routes. Contact counts nevertheless do not determine effective influence. That depends on transmission sign, cellular state, synaptic dynamics and behavioural context.

A single reconstructed animal also cannot establish the distribution of normal biological variation. Finding a motif repeatedly within that brain is not equivalent to comparing independent animals. For memory research, a further limitation is that a fixed structure does not show which connections changed after a particular experience or which changes were causally necessary.

Practical use should begin with a narrow reproducible query: specify the cell population, connection inclusion rules and annotation version, then examine sensitivity to weak contacts and segmentation errors. A proposed pathway should be compared with physiology or a perturbation capable of distinguishing it from neighbouring circuitry. This sequence makes the connectome a way to constrain models while leaving dynamics as an empirical question. For ORI, it is a valuable corrective to the temptation to treat a complete connection map as an already complete explanation of memory, behaviour or individual experience.
