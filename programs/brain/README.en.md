## Programme scope

The programme concerns the connections between circuit structure, neural activity and changes during learning. It covers cell types, connection maps, population dynamics and mechanisms of plasticity. Particular attention will go to the measurements needed to connect observed activity to memory, task conditions and behaviour.

## Programme questions

- **Circuit architecture.** What can cell types and connection maps reveal about a circuit's function?
- **Neural dynamics.** How do patterns of population activity represent stimuli, actions and past experience?
- **Memory and plasticity.** Which changes in cells and synapses support learning, retention and recall?

## First stage: information gathering

The next step is to gather and organise information. The proposed sequence is:

1. Define the scope of the first evidence map, including forms of memory, model organisms and levels of description.
2. Gather review articles and primary studies on circuit structure, activity and plasticity. Record the task, measured variables, interventions and methods used to test conclusions.
3. List open datasets and compare their connection maps, activity recordings, behavioural descriptions and temporal and spatial resolution.
4. Select one dataset and formulate a question for a future reproducible analysis, specifying methods and evaluation criteria.

## Planned materials

- An annotated bibliography on circuit architecture, neural dynamics and memory.
- An evidence map connecting structure, activity and learning, with the experimental context made explicit.
- A comparison of suitable open datasets and a proposal for the first analysis.
- After information gathering: a reproducible analysis of the selected data, with code, visualisations and a discussion of the result.
