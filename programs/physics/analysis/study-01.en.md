# Landauer — Irreversibility and Heat Generation in the Computing Process (1961)

[Русский](study-01.md) · [Programme](../README.en.md)

Checked: 25 October 2026. Initial source analysis; the indexed IBM/IEEE abstract was checked; direct DOI access required browser verification.

## Question and idea

Landauer examines operations whose outputs do not uniquely determine their inputs. The author abstract associates this logical irreversibility with physical irreversibility and heat generation. It also describes bistable-device models, switching speed and thermal errors. This is a theoretical paper, not an experimental measurement of a bound.

## What matters

Our interpretation is that an information-processing operation must be connected to its physical implementation. This does not imply identical energy costs for all computation or that contemporary electronics operate near a fundamental limit. Logical specification, implementation and measured device energy are different descriptive levels that require explicit connections.

## Critical questions

A careful assessment should specify the initial memory distribution, final task, environmental temperature and operations included in a cycle. Energy spent on preparation or control cannot silently disappear from an engineering account. The criterion for successful erasure also matters: rare errors and rapid execution change the practical problem. These questions are methodological implications of applying the paper, not a claim that we reproduced its calculations.

## Programme connection

The paper supplies a question that can be compared with Bérut and colleagues' experiment. This offers a model-to-measurement reading sequence; it does not show that one experiment covers all computing devices. For ORI, a useful next artifact is a correspondence table of operation, physical carrier, assumptions and observable. It should also separate the energy of a selected memory element from the cost of an entire computing system. No independent recalculation or full-text audit has been completed here.
