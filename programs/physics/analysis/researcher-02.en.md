## Researchers and scope

Minagawa's current author profile places him at Aix-Marseille Université and lists quantum measurement, resource theories and thermodynamics among his interests. Buscemi is affiliated with Nagoya University in the joint information-thermodynamics paper. His separate institutional page did not load during this check, so it does not provide a fresh confirmation of his current position.

## Main research line

The programme-relevant question is how to describe controllers and memory so that benefits from feedback are compared with the full protocol cost. This is theoretical research. It helps formulate measurement as a physical process but is not a report of a completed quantum device.

## ORI's analytical questions

Our proposed reading distinction is between permitted operations and available technology. Which transformations are mathematically allowed, which assumptions support the energy account, and what would an experimenter need to approach the ideal protocol? Even a rigorous result does not automatically specify the expense of every engineering implementation. These questions guide follow-up reading rather than assert deficiencies in the authors' work.

## Using this profile

Following specific papers and preprint versions is more informative than following the broad label “information.” Authorship should remain complete: the joint study also includes Mohammady, Sakai and Kato.
