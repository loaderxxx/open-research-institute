# Tickle, Martinez Arias, Placzek and Wolpert — Wolpert’s Principles of Development, 7th edition (2025)

[Русский](book-02.md)

**Access and status.** Checked 25 October 2026: indexed OUP description and contents; direct retrieval failed intermittently. Chapters were not read. A substantial dossier requires the full text.

The contents connect body-plan formation, morphogenesis, differentiation, organogenesis and regeneration. OUP also highlights new material on embryonic stem cells and embryo models. This scope provides a useful framework for ORI's question of how local cellular interactions generate tissue: form is situated within a developmental sequence rather than treated solely as a final image.

**ORI critical analysis.** Close reading should separate three claims: cells occupy a particular arrangement; a specific process generates that arrangement; changing that process changes the outcome. Observation establishes the first, temporal or mechanistic evidence supports the second, and controlled intervention tests the third. These levels are especially easy to conflate in morphogenesis, where compelling images can make a causal account seem more complete than it is.

Comparisons between organisms should follow operations rather than visual resemblance. A similar change in shape could arise through movement, proliferation or local changes in cell volume. The programme needs to identify measurements that discriminate between these explanations and determine whether they transfer to another tissue. Likewise, an embryo model should be assessed through reproducible functions and missing interactions, not merely its resemblance to an organism.

The book is a teaching synthesis. Its contents do not establish that any mechanism is universal or that the experimental literature is comprehensively represented. A productive ORI reading would choose one example of changing form and record its temporal sequence, environmental conditions and tests of signal necessity or sufficiency. A primary study with accessible data should then supply the evidential counterpart. Until this close reading occurs, specific arguments, page references and detailed accounts of individual experiments cannot responsibly be attributed to the authors. This limitation concerns the present analysis, not the potential value of the book itself.
