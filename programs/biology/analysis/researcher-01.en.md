# Alpha Yap — cell adhesion and epithelial mechanics

[Русский](researcher-01.md)

**Access.** Checked 24 October 2026: University of Queensland group profile, publication list and selected sections of Eckert et al. (2025). The institutional profile and stated example were inspected; the complete research corpus was not assessed.

The group investigates how epithelial cells transmit and sense mechanical influences while maintaining tissue integrity and responding to injury. Its institutional page identifies live microscopy, genetic manipulation in zebrafish embryos and physical approaches. A concrete collaborative example concerns orientational order on curved tissue surfaces; the publication list also includes a 2025 review of epithelial mechanoadaptation.

**ORI critical analysis.** This line deserves attention because it connects local junctions with barrier function. The relevant outcome should be stated at tissue level: whether integrity is preserved, how a response spreads and how neighbours compensate for damage. A molecular marker may support an explanation but cannot substitute for measuring the system's function.

Reading should distinguish force transmission, force sensing and the subsequent biochemical response. Disrupting a junction can affect all three simultaneously. Experiments that align mechanical measurements and functional responses in time are therefore particularly informative. The nematic-order study adds a way to describe organisation, but a geometrical quantity alone cannot identify the mechanism maintaining it. A review, meanwhile, helps map hypotheses rather than independently confirming every cited result.

For ORI, papers could be selected around a shared evidential pattern: local perturbation, neighbouring-cell response and changes in barrier function. This enables comparison across tissues without assuming that the same protein always has the same system-level role. Applicability remains limited by the particular epithelium, developmental stage and imaging conditions; transfer to other organs needs testing. Following the group means following its published research. It implies no collaboration, endorsement of ORI or external review of ORI materials. The programme's immediate task is to compare the evidence across scales, not to treat institutional reputation as a substitute for that comparison.
