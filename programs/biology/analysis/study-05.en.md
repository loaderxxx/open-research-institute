# Al-Hilal et al. (2025) — Durotaxis is a driver and potential therapeutic target in lung fibrosis and metastatic pancreatic cancer

[Русский](study-05.md)

**Access.** Checked 25 October 2026: Nature Cell Biology abstract and metadata through an indexed page; direct DOI retrieval failed through an authorisation redirect. Methods and supplements were not examined.

The authors report that migration along stiffness gradients contributes to lung fibrosis and metastatic pancreatic cancer in mouse models. Their proposed mechanism involves FAK–paxillin and mechanical signalling through YAP. The work moves durotaxis from a model surface into disease contexts, although the abstract alone cannot support independent assessment of all causal tests.

**ORI critical analysis.** The central distinction is between moving towards a stiffer environment and simply moving faster on a stiff substrate. Cell accumulation can also reflect proliferation, survival or retention. Evaluating the paper requires evidence that discriminates between these explanations. Spatial coincidence between cells and a stiff region does not itself measure directed migration.

A second issue is the independence of the mechanical variable. Tissue stiffness often changes alongside matrix composition and soluble signals. A persuasive causal account should therefore identify the part of the response attributable specifically to a mechanical gradient. Molecular intervention can likewise affect several cellular functions; reducing a pathological endpoint does not necessarily demonstrate selective suppression of durotaxis.

The study matters to ORI as an example of feedback between environment, movement and collective tissue state. A subsequent close reading should align stiffness measurements, cell trajectories, event timing and independent consequences of intervention. That would help determine whether mechanics is a directing influence or an associated feature of the process. A potential therapeutic target remains a research conclusion: mouse-model findings do not establish safety or effectiveness of human treatment. This entry offers no medical recommendation and does not claim examination of the complete experimental record. Its immediate purpose is to define the evidence needed to connect a physical tissue property with a specific cellular mechanism.
