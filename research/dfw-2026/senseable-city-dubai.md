This brief connects urban mobility studies with a proposed routing evaluation. Useful recommendations depend on when routes are accessible, what shade the model predicts along the route, and whether those predictions match what pedestrians encounter.

## Limitations

The full text of Walking smart in the heat was checked. Its outdoor comparison models the sunlit share of routes at four stations, not actual route choices or measured heat exposure. No new ORI route experiment has been run.
