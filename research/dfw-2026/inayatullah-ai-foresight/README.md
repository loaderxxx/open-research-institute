# Whose future does AI describe?

**English** · [Русский](README.ru.md)

Foresight is not prediction and the purpose of a workshop may be to preserve alternatives rather than converge on consensus. Causal Layered Analysis offers a means to separate visible claims from systemic causes, worldviews and metaphors. It does not make fluent AI output accurate, representative or authorized by participants. We propose a separate evaluation for all three.
