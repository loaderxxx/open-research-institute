# Lira Singularity: would we recognize a technological turning point?

[Русский](README.ru.md) · [English website](https://ori-lab.org/en/research/singularity/) · [Сайт на русском](https://ori-lab.org/ru/research/singularity/)

**Public research track · 19 November 2026 · literature appraisal and proposed protocol.** AI is already changing the work of developing AI. That is not the same as demonstrating that progress has become self-sustaining, irreversible or globally unpredictable. Lira Singularity asks what evidence would distinguish these possibilities, and which decisions a person can keep under their control in the meantime.

## Start with the claim you want to test

1. **Has progress accelerated?** A laboratory can measure how quickly its models improve on a fixed set of tasks. It cannot infer an economy-wide transition from that slope alone. [What the evidence shows, and why researchers disagree](EVIDENCE.md).
2. **Can we anticipate a change that matters?** A forecast needs a deadline and a source that can settle it. We propose comparing dated probabilities with a simple historical baseline before using them to guide a decision. [Forecast protocol and worked scoring example](FORECAST.md).
3. **What remains under human control?** A team can measure time saved *after review*, retain a fallback, and decide who can stop a workflow. These steps can help whether progress accelerates or stalls. [Adaptation and human controls](ADAPTATION.md).

**What would count as stronger evidence?** Several independent groups would need to document successive rounds in which improvements attributable to AI measurably accelerate the next round, after accounting for compute, staffing, task changes and human intervention. Even that would support a feedback mechanism, not by itself establish irreversibility. No finite run of increasing scores proves the trend cannot reverse.

**Release boundary.** ORI has not run the proposed forecasting pilot, compared live forecasters, measured local productivity, or demonstrated a date for a singularity. No automatic actions follow from this research.
