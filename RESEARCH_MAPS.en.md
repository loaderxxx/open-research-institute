# Research programme literature maps

[Русский](RESEARCH_MAPS.md)

Initial collections of public sources for all 13 Open Research Institute programmes. Collection date: **24 October 2026**. Each map covers books, the history of the field, research publications, researchers and teams, current questions and a suggested sequence for further reading.

The collections contain **39 book recommendations, 66 publications and research resources, and 39 researcher or team entries**. Entries are counted by programme: a book, publication or team may appear in several maps. Each programme has three historical milestones and three books to begin reading.

## How to use this collection

- **Books** provide conceptual and historical foundations. A public publisher listing does not imply free access to the complete book. Access is indicated alongside the source.
- **Publications and data** were selected for their relevance to programme questions. Reviews, primary studies, preprints and datasets serve different purposes; annotations state limitations.
- **Researchers and teams** are starting points for following their work. This is neither a ranking nor a claim of affiliation with the institute. Recency is supported by dated work and public profiles.
- **Current research directions** reflect materials located and checked, not every publication available at the collection date. A verified 2024 or 2025 source does not become a 2026 publication because of its verification date.

This is a targeted preliminary collection, not a systematic review, a full reading of every source or an independent replication. Maps state access and reading depth; annotations were prepared with AI assistance. Substantive decisions require reading original sources and comparing independent evidence. Complete third-party books and articles are not copied into the repository.

## Next stage

For each programme, choose one specific question, refine search criteria, read available full texts and build an evidence table covering the question, method, data, result and limitations. Then prepare a review or a reproducible analysis plan. See the [methodology](METHODOLOGY.en.md) for details.

Literature maps are counted separately from the [21 existing reviews](LIBRARY.en.md). Adding them does not make the six developing programmes completed studies. Existing `SOURCES.md` files record sources and reading depth for earlier reviews; the new maps supplement them.
