# Editorial Note

[Русский](EDITORIAL_NOTE.md)

The corpus was prepared with AI assistance for searching, extraction, comparison, and editing. This is internal review, not external scientific peer review. The initial short register was checked on 22 September 2026; the expanded library was prepared and selectively checked on 23 September 2026. Reading depth varies: some records are based on an abstract or an available excerpt. The register preserves the specific depth for each source.

A retracted article is mentioned only with its retraction identified. Corrections and later responses may change an interpretation. Links to primary sources allow readers to check the account. Facts reported by authors do not mean that our initiative repeated their experiments. A preprint is not the same as a peer-reviewed article; a philosophical position is not the same as an empirical result.

The public release contains only reviews of work already published, together with necessary qualifications. Corrections can be proposed through Issues by identifying the record, primary source, and suggested change. Do not submit personal data or confidential material in public discussions.
